8517.13.00 — Smartphones, for cellular networks
MFN duty: 3.7% · Luxembourg VAT: 17% · CBAM: No
Classification rationale: Smartphones fall under CN heading 8517, specifically subheading 8517.13 for "Telephones for cellular networks or for other wireless networks" with the further subdivision .00 for the 8-digit CN code used in EU tariff declarations. This classification is consistent with WCO HS explanatory notes and ECJ case law on smartphones.
