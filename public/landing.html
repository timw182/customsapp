Section A · Statement of purpose

Customs duty. Excise. VAT. Mobile & Excise, on one shelf.

§ A.1The case in brief

Dutify is the customs landed‑cost bureau for the European Union — two engines under one roof. Mobile classifies your goods to CN8/TARIC10 with AI verified against the EU reference, applies Incoterms 2020 to customs value per UCC Article 70, looks up the destination VAT across all 27 member states, and flags FTA preference where it applies. Excise calculates duty on spirits, beer, wine, intermediate products, tobacco and energy at the live rates of each member state — hectolitres of pure alcohol, °Plato, sticks, kilograms, gigajoules, whatever the schedule asks for. Audit trail you can hand to a broker.

§ 06 · Foundations

What both engines rest on.

§ 06.1
Live TARIC verification
Every classification is checked against the EU's TARIC reference at lookup time. CN10 codes are probed level by level. No stale yearly snapshots — if Brussels changed it this morning, you see it this morning.
Source · DG TAXUD
§ 06.2
UCC Article 70
Customs value is the transaction value, adjusted for freight and insurance to the EU border. Dutify keeps to the Union Customs Code: no ad‑hoc add‑ons, no "we usually do it this way". The basis is cited on every receipt.
Ref · UCC Reg. 952/2013
§ 06.3
Built in Luxembourg
Luxembourg sits at a useful intersection — three borders, three languages, every fortnight a customs update. Dutify is built here, serves the whole EU, and treats every member state's schedule as a peer of the others — no centralism, no anglocentrism.
