Four fields. Total in seconds.

landed cost · audit-able
A
Field A · Classify

Describe the goods

Plain language in EN or FR. Claude returns a candidate CN10, we round-trip to TARIC for verification.

B
Field B · Origin & tax

Pick origin · destination

VAT auto-suggests from EU27 TEDB. Trade-preference chips (GSP, EFTA, EPA…) appear automatically for the chosen origin.
