§ 01

What is in the dossier

Every tool a customs broker reaches for, on one surface
N° 01 · Tariff

MFN & preferential rates, TARIC-verified

Every rate is queried live against the EU TARIC SOAP service. Anti-dumping, countervailing, safeguards, and suspensions all surface, with expired measures filtered.

N° 02 · HS Lookup

AI classification with CN10 probing

Claude proposes the HS6 heading, then the system probes down three levels — subheading → CN8 → CN10 — verifying each is declarable, and returning siblings for disambiguation.

N° 03 · Excise

Luxembourg excise — schema-driven

Alcohol, tobacco, and energy products calculated from ADA-published rates. Each category carries its own schema — inputs, formula, VAT tier — updated automatically every 14 days.

N° 04 · CBAM

Carbon Border Adjustment, priced

Chapters under the EU CBAM regulation are flagged automatically, with embedded emissions converted to indicative certificate cost at the prevailing EU ETS price.

N° 05 · T1 Transit

Draft T1 declarations in a dossier PDF

Principal, consignor, consignee, transport, guarantee, and goods — drafted to the EU DVA format and exported as a dossier-styled PDF ready for review.

N° 06 · Sanity

FX & sanctions, checked at entry

ECB reference rates refresh daily; sanctioned origins and restricted goods are flagged before you complete the calculation.

Live · TARIC verified

No more dead-end HS codes.

Classifying a product often produces a heading that isn't actually declarable — it needs a CN8 or CN10 subdivision. Dutify probes automatically and refuses to return a code that customs would reject. When it's ambiguous, it tells you why and shows you the siblings.
