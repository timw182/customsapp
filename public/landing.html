04 · Foundations

What both engines rest on.

04.1
Live TARIC verification
Every classification is checked against the EU's TARIC reference at lookup time. CN10 codes are probed level by level — no stale yearly snapshots. If Brussels changed it this morning, you see it this morning.
Source · DG TAXUD
04.2
UCC Article 70
Customs value is the transaction value, adjusted for freight and insurance to the EU border. Dutify keeps to the Union Customs Code — no ad‑hoc add‑ons. The basis is cited on every receipt.
Ref · UCC Reg. 952/2013
Built in Luxembourg
04.3
Three borders, three languages, a customs update every fortnight. Dutify is built here, serves the whole EU, and treats every member state's schedule as a peer — no centralism, no anglocentrism.
